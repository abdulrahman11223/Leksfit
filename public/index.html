The tag on every piece

Every stitch is checked before it leaves the shop.

LEKSFIT Signature is run by Lekan Muyi — a Kwara-based fashion designer who trained as a tailor before he ever picked up a hashtag. Nothing here is mass-produced. Fabric is chosen by hand, embroidery is placed deliberately, and every finished piece is signed with the LEKSFIT tag before it goes out.

Whether it's a full agbada for a wedding or a simple senator for Friday service, the standard doesn't change.
